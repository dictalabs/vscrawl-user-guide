# Sign In

## Accessing the Login Page
Go to: [vScrawl Login](https://app.vscrawl.com/sign-in?redirectURL=%2Fdocuments%2Fall-documents)

## Enter Credentials

- Enter your **registered email address** or **username**.
    
- Type your **password** (case-sensitive).
    
- Click **Sign In** to access your **vScrawl** account.

![vScrawl Signin](../images/signin-screen.png)
## Signup / Sign-in using Google Authentication or Keycloak

You can sign in using either **Google authentication** or **Key cloak authentication**. On the login page, simply choose **“Continue with Google”** and select your Google account. If prompted, enter your Google password.

Alternatively, if your organization uses Key cloak, click **“Continue with Key cloak”** and enter your Key cloak username and password. Once authenticated, you will be redirected to the **vScrawl** **Dashboard**.

## Signup / Sign-in with the Smart Card Using the SmartXipher App

vScrawl provides secure authentication through **Smart Card login** using the **SmartXipher App**. This ensures strong identity verification and protects access to your account.

When a user chooses to sign in with a **Smart Card**, the system verifies their **digital certificate** through **SmartXipher**. Once the **certificate** is validated, the user is securely logged into their **dashboard.**

![vScrawl Signin](../images/smart-card-login.png)

## Sign-in Using Passkey

To sign in to vScrawl using a passkey, first open the login page and enter your registered email address, then click **“Login with Passkey.”** A dialog titled **“Sign in using a saved passkey”** will appear, allowing you to choose how you want to authenticate. You can select **Windows Hello or an external security key** to sign in using your device’s fingerprint, face recognition, PIN, or a USB security key. 

Alternatively, you can choose **Use a phone or tablet**, which will display a QR code on the screen; scan this QR code using your mobile phone and approve the sign-in using your device’s biometric authentication or screen lock. Once the authentication is successfully completed, you will be automatically logged in to your vScrawl account without entering a password.

![vScrawl Signin](../images/pass-key-login.png)
